How we got to 4,500 credits a year

Every step is verified by India's Offset Mechanism methodologies.

1

1. Measure the trees and biomass

BM-T-AR-0004

Field surveyors recorded tree diameter (DBH) and height across 34 sample plots. The calculation engine applied species-specific wood densities and allometric curves to determine aboveground and belowground living biomass.

2

2. Compare with doing nothing (baseline)

BM-T-001

Demonstrated additionality via barrier analysis. Without this project, the land was degraded grassland with constant baseline carbon stock, yielding zero baseline sequestration.

3

3. Subtract side effects (leakage)

BM-T-AR-0005

Checked for displacement of grazing and agricultural activities. Zero leakage was confirmed because planting occurred on non-agricultural community land without displacing existing farm output.

4

4. Credits you can claim

BM FR05.002

Gross removal of 5,000 t CO₂e minus a 10% non-permanence safety buffer (500 t CO₂e) results in 4,500 Carbon Credit Certificates (CCCs) ready for issuance.
